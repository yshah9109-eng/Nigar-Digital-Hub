NIGAR DIGITAL HUB — SEO LAUNCH CHECKLIST

1. Replace https://your-domain.com/ in sitemap.xml and robots.txt with the real website domain.
2. After the real domain is known, add an absolute canonical URL to every HTML page.
3. Update the Open Graph image/URL metadata with the production URL.
4. Deploy the site on a public HTTPS domain that returns HTTP 200 for each page.
5. Verify the domain in Google Search Console.
6. Submit /sitemap.xml in Search Console.
7. Use URL Inspection in Search Console to request indexing for the homepage and important pages.
8. Keep internal links working and use descriptive link text.
9. Replace placeholder project/team/review content with genuine information.
10. Add real social profile links when available.
11. Keep page titles and descriptions unique and natural; do not keyword-stuff.
12. Publish useful, original content regularly and earn relevant links/mentions from other websites.

IMPORTANT: No website can honestly be guaranteed a #1 Google ranking. SEO improves eligibility, understanding, visibility and competitiveness, but Google ranking depends on many factors including relevance, quality, competition, links, location and user intent.
